Privacy Policy: Tidoo Wars: Tower Defense

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Tidoo Wars: Tower Defense ("we", "us", "the app") respects your privacy. This document explains what data the app collects, why, and your rights regarding it.

The game itself needs no account and keeps your progress (levels, stars, coins, medals, armory and settings) only on your device. We run no servers of our own and never see your progress. The data below is handled by the advertising and purchase services built into the Android app.

1. Data we collect

Category Source Why it is collected
Device or other IDs (the Android advertising ID and an anonymous app user ID) Google AdMob, RevenueCat To serve and measure rewarded ads, prevent ad fraud, and link your ad-free purchase to this install
Approximate location (derived from your IP address) Google AdMob To serve ads suited to your region and to prevent fraud
App interactions (ads loaded, shown and tapped) Google AdMob, RevenueCat To measure ads, pay out ad revenue and prevent fraud
Diagnostics (device model, OS version, app version, ad performance) Google AdMob To keep ads working and to detect fraud
Purchase history (the one-time Ad-free pack) Google Play, RevenueCat To unlock and restore the Ad-free pack
Ad consent choice (Europe, the UK and similar regions) Google User Messaging Platform To remember whether you agreed to personalised ads

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera input, or precise location.

2. How we use your data

  • Ads: rewarded videos are optional; you choose when to watch one. AdMob uses the data above to show the ad, count it and pay for it.
  • Purchases: RevenueCat and Google Play check whether you own the Ad-free pack, so the game can skip ad videos and restore the pack on a new phone.
  • Consent: in regions where the law requires it, the game asks for your ad consent before any ad is requested, and remembers your answer. You can change it any time under Credits → Privacy choices.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. The services below receive data from the app:

  • Google AdMob (Google LLC) uses the device and ad data above for advertising, ad measurement and fraud prevention, including for its own purposes as described in its policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google User Messaging Platform (Google LLC) stores your ad consent choice: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.) processes purchase records, the anonymous app user ID and ad event data on our behalf: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Google Play (Google LLC) processes the payment for the Ad-free pack: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Your rights

You can:

  • Reset or delete your Android advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads, depending on your phone).
  • Change your ad consent choice in the game under Credits → Privacy choices (shown in regions where the consent form applies).
  • Delete your game progress by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage.
  • Ask us to delete the purchase and ad records linked to your anonymous app user ID, or to send you a copy, by emailing philanthropist91@gmail.com.

We act on deletion requests within 30 days.

5. Security

All data the app sends travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Your game progress stays in the app's private storage on your device.

6. Children

Tidoo Wars is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app and data was collected, email philanthropist91@gmail.com and we will ask the services above to delete it.

7. International transfers / GDPR

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland: the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are requested, and that consent is the legal basis for advertising data. Purchase records are processed to perform the contract (delivering the Ad-free pack you bought). We operate from Pakistan; Google and RevenueCat process data in the United States and other countries. You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time (Credits → Privacy choices). Email philanthropist91@gmail.com. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

8. California residents / CCPA

The app shares device identifiers and ad interaction data with Google AdMob for advertising, which California law may treat as "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising. We do not sell personal information for money. You can opt out of personalised ads by resetting or deleting your advertising ID, or turning off ad personalisation in your phone's settings. You can ask to know, delete or correct your information by emailing philanthropist91@gmail.com. We will not discriminate against you for using these rights.

9. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our practices change. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent change.

10. Contact
