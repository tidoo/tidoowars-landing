Why Tidoo Wars

Tanks roll in, speeders race past, and at the end of the campaign the giant Tidoo Titan marches down the road. Pick the right towers, place them well, and hold the line, in quick battles made for your phone.

5 towers, 3 upgrades each

Machine Gun, Rocket Launcher, Slow Tower, Sniper Tower and Power Tower. Upgrade them mid-battle with the gold you earn.

Boss titans

Titans soak up fire, and the Tidoo Titan, the biggest of them all, costs 10 hearts if it reaches your core.

The Armory

Spend coins and medals from your wins to buy towers, research each one 5 times, then unlock its legendary upgrade.

Campaign and endless

10 levels on grass and snow battlefields. Finish a map to unlock endless mode on it and see how long you last.

Abilities for tight moments

Call an Artillery Strike, lay a Minefield, or push every tower into Overdrive.

Ads only when you choose

Optional rewarded videos give a second chance, double coins or a supply drop. A one-time Ad-free pack gives the rewards without the video.
